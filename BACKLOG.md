## Principles

Hard-won. Change them only with a reason.

1. **Plan before animating.** Film 1 was rebuilt many times because its shape was
   discovered while animating. `/new-film` stops at the beat sheet on purpose.
2. **Verify on rendered frames.** Never claim a visual result you have not looked at.
3. **The pixel-diff is the contract for any refactor.** If `tools/pixdiff.js` says
   frames changed and you did not intend them to, the refactor is wrong — not the
   tool.
4. **Promote to engine on second use, not on first guess.** A drawing helper stays in
   the film that needs it until a second film needs it too. Guessing what is reusable
   is how engines get full of things nobody calls.
5. **Assert every scripted edit.** A silent no-op replace once left a card rendering
   over a screen for several handoffs.
6. **Say what cannot be done.** Three failed attempts at folded arms were worth more
   than one silent substitution.
7. **No number on screen that cannot be traced to a source.**

### BED-CURVE-CONFIG — the audio engine hardcodes film 1's beat names
`engine/audio.js` reads `AT["l1head"]`, `AT["tomeme"]` and `AT["memeup"]` directly to
decide when the music steps down, drops out and returns. A second film with different
beat names gets `undefined` and a bed that never changes level.

The module header already documents the intended contract (`BED_CURVE` declared by
the film) but it is not implemented. Make the film declare:

```js
const BED_CURVE = {quiet: "l1head", out: "tomeme", back: "memeup"};
```

and have `bedLevel()` read that, falling back to a flat bed when it is absent.

### AUDIO-NO-CLIP — a film with no video clip cannot build
`audioGraph()` calls `createMediaElementSource(vid)` unconditionally, and `syncMeme`
assumes the element exists. Film 1 has a clip; the next one may not.

Guard both on `vid` being present, and make `media.html` optional in `tools/build.py`.
