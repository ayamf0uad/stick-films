# Film 1 — My Claude Code journey

Retro-fitted after the fact. Film 1 was made before `/new-film` existed; this is what
its brief would have said.

| | |
|---|---|
| length | 4:10 (cap was 5:00) |
| audience | the whole company, async, in Slack |
| sound | music bed + one video clip with its own audio; must also read muted |
| cast | the hero (blue), Claude (orange) |

## The story in one sentence

I wanted to understand Claude Code, so I built a real product with it, and here is
what it taught me and what got built.

## Tone

Wry and self-deprecating, but the content is technical and credible. Explicitly not
salesy — this is not a pitch. Never "I'm just a QA"; QAs are capable.

## Shape

| act | beats | what it does |
|---|---|---|
| opening | walk in, wave, title | who is talking |
| the desk | bored, remembered, need a project, the idea, project locked | why this happened |
| how I started | CLAUDE.md, TDD, watched it work, tested the output | the setup |
| three lessons | a card, an exchange with Claude, takeaways — three times | the actual content |
| 3am | the meme clip, both figures dancing | the joke that earns the ending |
| what got built | walks the funnel · every finding is a chain · under the hood · same page in, same findings out · curtain | the payoff |

## Rules that shaped it

- No claims about integrating with Convert — not synced with the devs.
- No commit counts or test counts — not relevant to a wider audience.
- No em dashes in on-screen copy.
- No numbers that cannot be traced. "Verified across 12 runs" is on screen only
  because Aya stated it.
- Don't use the tool's own annotated screenshots — that work was parked as inaccurate.

## Real material used

The Printfresh run: the funnel steps, the P1 promo-code root cause with its evidence
problem, hypothesis and experiment, and the deterministic findings. The funnel and
the finding chain were **redrawn** rather than screenshotted — the real screenshots
rendered at about ten pixels of text and could not be read.
